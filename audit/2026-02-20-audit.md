| 2026-02-20 | 0 logged | Alerts may have stopped attempting |

**Root cause:** Discord role mentions `<@&697950067285295115>` in `raw_message` are passed to Telegram's HTML parser as-is. The `_sanitize_html()` function in `alerts/telegram.py` escapes `<`/`>` but the function apparently isn't applied to the message content being sent.

**Fix:** Strip `<@&\d+>` and `<@\d+>` patterns from message text before sending.
