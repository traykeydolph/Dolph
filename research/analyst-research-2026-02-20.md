## 🔧 TECHNICAL NOTES FOR BOT INTEGRATION

### Telegram Groups
- Need a Telegram user account or bot to monitor channels
- Cornix-formatted signals (UCS, MYC) follow a standard template:
  ```
  📊 #BTC/USDT
  Signal Type: Long
  Entry: 95,000 - 95,500
  Leverage: 10x
  
  TP1: 96,200
  TP2: 97,500  
  TP3: 99,000
  
  SL: 93,800
  ```
- Non-Cornix groups still use structured text but formats vary slightly

### Discord Groups
- Already have Discord monitoring infrastructure from ECS/Grizzlies
- FRF and Axion should work with similar parsing logic
- Look for dedicated signal channels (not general chat)

### Cornix Bot Consideration
- Multiple recommended groups support Cornix auto-trading
- Could potentially use Cornix as middleware instead of custom parsing
- Cornix supports Binance, Bybit, OKX futures
- Cost: Cornix itself is ~$20-30/mo

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT CAVEATS

1. **Win rate claims are often inflated.** Independent tests show most groups perform 20-30% below their claimed rates. Budget for ~60-70% actual win rate even from "90%+" groups
2. **No group has fully verified performance.** CryptoNinjas comes closest with public Google Sheets
3. **High-leverage signals (10x+) carry liquidation risk.** Our bot should cap leverage or have hard limits
4. **Free tiers are limited.** Most give 2-5 signals/week free vs. daily for paid
5. **Telegram monitoring requires different infrastructure** than our Discord bot. Will need to add Telegram client library (e.g., Telethon for Python)
6. **Always paper trade new signal sources for 2-4 weeks** before going live
